# Results → figures and tables

`checks/` holds the frozen comparison targets; `figures/*/source_data/` holds the aggregates the
drawing scripts read.

Drawing scripts are included for Figure 1, Figure 3, Figure S3, Figure S4 and Figure S10. The other
artefacts are not generated by scripts in this repository.

### T7 coverage

The frozen `T7 Neutrophil state tests.csv` has 813 rows:

| Block | Rows | Reproduced here |
|---|---:|---|
| `cell_type` level, unpaired, 7 subtypes × 2 contrasts × 51 metrics | 714 | No — needs per-subtype pseudobulk that is not in this release |
| `neutrophil paired states`, aggregated immature vs mature, 51 metrics | 51 | Yes — `neutrophil_paired` |
| `neutrophil paired states`, 4 subtype-vs-mature comparisons × 12 metrics (3 tested) | 48 | No — same missing input |

The frozen `fdr_global` and `fdr_within_metric` columns are BH families over all 381 tested rows of
that table. Because 330 of those rows cannot be recomputed here, the FDR columns are not re-derived;
`neutrophil_paired` checks `n`, medians, the median paired difference and the paired Wilcoxon `p`
(306 ledger rows).
